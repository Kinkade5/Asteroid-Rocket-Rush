Rocket Rush — Privacy Policy

Effective July 15, 2026 · applies to the Rocket Rush web game and its Android app
THE SHORT VERSION
  • No accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking, no cookies.
  • Your game progress lives on your own device.
  • The only thing ever sent to us: a nickname and score, if you choose to join the public leaderboard.
  • We don't sell or share data with anyone.

What this covers

Rocket Rush is a small arcade game made by an independent developer. This policy explains what information the game handles, where it goes, and what your choices are. It applies to the game at asteroidrocketrush.netlify.app and to the same game installed from Google Play (which is this website packaged as an app).

Data stored on your device only

The game saves your progress in your browser's local storage, on your device. This includes your scores and bests, coins and stars, purchased upgrades and skins, selected difficulty, audio and accessibility settings, and the leaderboard nickname you chose. None of this leaves your device, except the leaderboard submission described below. Deleting the app or clearing the site's browsing data removes it all.

The global leaderboard (optional)

If you achieve a qualifying score, the game offers to post it to the public leaderboard. This is optional — if you never enter a nickname and submit, nothing is ever sent. When you do submit, we store exactly four things on the game's server:

  • the nickname you typed (3–12 letters/numbers; a filter blocks offensive names),
  • your score,
  • your rounds (asteroids passed),
  • the date and time of the submission.

Leaderboard entries are public — every player can see the nickname and score. Please use a made-up nickname, not your real name. We never ask for your name, email, or any other personal details, and there is no way to attach them to a score even if you wanted to.

Hosting & server logs

The game and its leaderboard are hosted on Netlify. Like virtually all web hosting, Netlify's infrastructure briefly processes technical data such as IP addresses and browser type to deliver the site and defend against abuse. We don't access or store this ourselves; see Netlify's privacy policy for how they handle it.

Children

Rocket Rush is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. The only user-provided input in the entire game is the leaderboard nickname, which is filtered, is not required to play, and should never be a real name.

What we don't do

  • No advertising, and no ad networks or SDKs.
  • No analytics or tracking of any kind.
  • No cookies.
  • No accounts, sign-ins, or contact details.
  • No selling, renting, or sharing of any data with third parties.

Removing your data

Leaderboard entry: email us the nickname and difficulty tier and we'll remove it. Everything else is on your own device — delete the app, or clear this site's data in your browser settings, and it's gone.

Changes

If the game ever starts handling data differently (for example, a future feature), this policy will be updated here first, with a new effective date, before that feature ships.
